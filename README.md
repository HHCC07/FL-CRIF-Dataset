## Data Access

This dataset follows a shared-access model: **annotations are publicly available, while source images and videos are provided upon request and approval**.

### Public Annotations

The following annotation files are available directly in this GitHub repository without an access request:

- YOLO-format bounding-box annotations for vehicle detection.
- YOLO-format bounding-box annotations for headlight/glare detection.
- Vehicle-body and headlight/glare MOT annotations for five roadside camera scenes.

### Requesting Source Images and Videos

Source images and MOT videos are distributed through a request-based review process to respect the rights associated with the recordings and prevent unauthorized commercial use or redistribution.

Please send the following information **from your university institutional email address** to [gr0796er@ed.ritsumei.ac.jp](mailto:gr0796er@ed.ritsumei.ac.jp).

```text
Subject: FL-CRIF Source Image and Video Access Request

Name:
University:
Department or research group:
University institutional email:
Research project title:
Research purpose and intended use:
Requested images, video sequences, or data subsets:

I confirm that the requested images and videos will be used only
for the non-commercial academic research described above.
I will acknowledge the dataset and cite the associated paper
in resulting publications.

Without separate written authorization, I will not sell,
redistribute, publicly upload, or use the supplied images
and videos in commercial products or services.

I agree to the applicable data-use conditions.
```

We will review the applicant’s affiliation, intended research use, and applicable permissions for sharing the requested materials. Following approval and confirmation of the use conditions, the approved images and videos will be provided through a private download link or another agreed transfer method.

### Use and Citation

Please acknowledge the dataset and cite the associated paper when publishing research results obtained using these materials.

Source images and videos provided through the application process are restricted to the approved non-commercial academic research. Selling, publicly uploading, redistributing, or using these materials in commercial products or services requires separate written authorization.
